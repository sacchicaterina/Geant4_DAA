# Geant4_DAA
Geant4 project for DAA 

n8 
Simulate the interaction of 12C of 120 MeV/u kinetic energy with a volume of water
reconstructing the Bragg peak.
Try to check differences in the obtained results from using different physics lists
among the available ones

n5
Simulate the interaction of protons of 100 MeV kinetic energy with a volume of water
reconstructing the Bragg peak.
Try to check differences in the obtained results from using different physics lists
among the available ones
